Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

Visit statistics

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your browser's address and basic details such as device type and rough location. It does not tell me who you are. Google's own use of that data is described in Google's privacy policy, and you can opt out with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Pitch me a job

If you send a job through the Pitch me a job page, what you type - your name, how to reach you, the job description and its link - is stored in my own database and read by my AI agent, which uses Anthropic's Claude to compare the role with my profile. I use it only to consider the role and reply to you. It is never sold or shared for marketing.

Everything else

There are no accounts on this site, and nothing else you do here is recorded beyond the hosting provider's standard server logs.

Questions

To ask about or remove anything you sent, message me on LinkedIn.
